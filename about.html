How we work.
And who you're working with.

No scraper dressed up as intelligence. Public signals, read by hand, turned into a shortlist your reps can use the same day.

Who we are

We read the signals a company leaves in public and turn them into a shortlist your reps can open a call with.

Who you're working with

Your brief goes to one analyst, working across Europe, from first read to final list, with no handoffs and no offshore queue in between. The person who reads your brief is the person who builds it. That's deliberate: judgement doesn't outsource well.

What we won't do

We won't invent a buying signal to pad a list, and we won't pass off a scrape as a relationship. If the fit isn't there, we tell you instead of filling the gap with noise. Every signal we cite traces back to a public source you could check yourself.

The method

From raw signal
to plain English.

Four steps, one throughline: at every stage we turn data into language a person can actually read and act on.

01
With you · 30 minutes

We define the fit in your words.

No filter forms or checkbox builders. You describe your ideal customer in a sentence, and we work from that, the way you'd brief a colleague.

02
Argus · Lead Lists

We research the signals.

Argus surfaces the accounts that are actually moving, then writes out why, so a fit score never arrives without a reason in plain language.

03
Lynx · Account Intelligence

We map the account.

For priority accounts, Lynx builds the picture an SDR can't pull from a CRM, and describes the way in as a person would, not as an org chart to decode.

04
72-hour SLA

You get it paste-ready.

Every line reads like a human wrote it, because the whole point is language you can use, not a dashboard you have to interpret.
