How we work.
And who you're working with.

Who we are

Anyone can find public data. The work is reading it, and knowing which fragments add up to a company worth calling now.

Who you're working with

Your brief is followed by one person, from first read to final list. The person who reads your brief is the one who writes your report. It stays with one person because judgement is hard to hand off.

What we won't do

We won't invent a buying signal to pad a list, and we won't hand you a scraped list and call it research. Every account on your list is there for a reason we can show you.

The method

From scattered traces
to a reason to call.

The public web is full of fragments. It takes four steps to turn them into something worth acting on: we collect, connect, weigh and interpret, so what reaches you is already a judgement, not a data set. Behind it all is our own tool: we run it, so you don't have to learn it.

01
With you · 30 minutes

We work out who you want in your words.

No filter forms or checkbox builders. You describe your ideal customer in a sentence, and we work from that, the way you'd brief a colleague.

02
ARGUS · Lead Lists

We turn data into signals.

A job posting, a funding round and a new hire mean little on their own. We cross-reference them, weigh them against the profile you gave us and read what they add up to: this company is moving, and this is why now is the moment.

03
LYNX · Account Intelligence

We reconstruct the whole picture.

On priority accounts we go further: who really decides, who influences the budget, where the opening is. It's the picture no CRM holds, because it doesn't exist anywhere until someone builds it.

04
A matter of days

You get the answer, not the homework.

What lands in ARGUS and LYNX is the conclusion of the analysis, not its raw material: which companies, why now, who to speak to and how to open. The interpretation is already done, and we explain it when we hand it over.
