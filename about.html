How we work.
And who you're working with.

Who we are

We read the signals a company leaves in public and turn them into a shortlist your reps can open a call with.

Who you're working with

Your brief is followed by one person, from first read to final list. The person who reads your brief is the one who writes your report. That's deliberate: judgement doesn't outsource well.

What we won't do

We won't invent a buying signal to pad a list, and we won't hand you a data dump with a score on top. Every account on your list is there because the analysis says it belongs.

The method

From scattered traces
to a reason to call.

The public web is full of fragments. Four steps to turn them into something worth acting on: we collect, connect, weigh and interpret, and what reaches you is already a judgement, not a data set.

01
With you · 30 minutes

We define the fit in your words.

No filter forms or checkbox builders. You describe your ideal customer in a sentence, and we work from that, the way you'd brief a colleague.

02
Argus · Lead Lists

We turn data into signals.

A job posting, a funding round and a new hire mean little on their own. We cross-reference them, weigh them against your ICP and read what they add up to: this company is moving, and this is why now is the moment.

03
Lynx · Account Intelligence

We reconstruct the whole picture.

On priority accounts we go further: who really decides, who influences the budget, where the opening is. It's the picture no CRM holds, because it doesn't exist anywhere until someone builds it.

04
A matter of days

You get the answer, not the homework.

What lands in ARGUS and LYNX is the conclusion of the analysis, not its raw material: which companies, why now, who to speak to and how to open. The interpretation is already done, and we explain it when we hand it over.

What we
guarantee.

Very short turnaround, a matter of days.

From the moment you confirm the brief, the list reaches you in a matter of days, and we explain it when we hand it over.

Every reading comes with its source.

If we tell you a company has just won a public tender, the link to the award notice is right there next to it.

Questions answered for 30 days.

After delivery, you can ask the person who wrote your report anything about it, for 30 days.

A reply within 24 hours.

Whatever you write to us, someone answers the same day or the next.

A flat fee,
no credit counter.

When you subscribe you pay a flat fee, with your reports included. There's no credit counter to watch, and no surprise at the end of the month.

What moves the number

Whether you need Argus, Lynx or both, how wide your market is, and how many reports a month your team can actually work.

Three separate licences

ARGUS, LYNX and COLLAB are bought separately. If you already have a list, you only need LYNX. Collab is a workspace of its own, not the place your reports are delivered.

How we get to a number

Tell us who you're looking for and what you're trying to hit. We reply within 24 hours, talk it through on a call, and you decide before anything is committed.
