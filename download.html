Installing takes three steps

Download

Pick the version for your computer and wait for the file to finish.

Install

On Mac, open the .dmg and drag SIFT into Applications. On Windows, run the .exe and follow the installer.

Sign in

Open SIFT and sign in with the account that came with your licence.
