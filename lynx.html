The analysis

One company, and the people who could shape the deal.

Every analysis covers the same ground: what the company does, what had changed before the report, who it competes with, and a working hypothesis of the deal: who might back you, who might weigh in, who might sign off the spend.

  • Each person's likely part in the deal, set out as a scenario
  • News from the 90 days before your report, each with its source and date
  • Moirai, the chat that takes the analysis further
