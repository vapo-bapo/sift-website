Where the value is

We don't find data.
We read it.

The raw material is public: anyone can see a hiring page or a funding round. The value is in what those fragments mean together. We connect them, weigh them, and turn them into a buying signal, written in plain language: why this company, why now, and where to start.

What we stand behind

Promises we'll
put in writing.

Free re-run

If more than 20% of a list falls outside your agreed ICP, we re-run it at no charge. You tell us what's off, we go back to the research.

Interpreted by an analyst

A person reads every list and every brief before it ships, and decides what the signals actually mean for your deal. Nothing leaves here as raw data.

Our reading, your evidence

Anyone can find a job posting. The work is knowing which ones mean budget is moving. We do that reading for you, and cite the evidence behind it so you can defend the call in a meeting.

72 hours, scheduled

A confirmed brief turns into a delivered shortlist in three days, slotted on the calendar and not sitting in a queue.

A hiring page is data. Eleven engineers hired six weeks after a new sales director arrived is a signal. Turning the first into the second is the work we do: reading scattered public traces and reconstructing what they mean for your deal.

Who you're working with

Your brief goes to one analyst, working across Europe, from first read to final list, with no handoffs and no offshore queue in between. The person who reads your brief is the person who builds it.

What we won't do

We won't invent a buying signal to pad a list, and we won't hand you a data dump with a score on top. If the signals don't add up to a real opportunity, we say so instead of filling the gap with noise.
