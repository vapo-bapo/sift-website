We don't find data.

A company that filed a capital increase last month has money to spend, and usually a plan it hasn't announced yet. The raw material is public. The value is in what the fragments mean together, so we put them side by side, decide which ones matter for you, and write it down in plain language: why this company, why now, and where to start.
