Lead Lists

Argus

Argus builds precision lead lists from your ICP alone, no generic scraping. It watches hiring, funding, leadership, and tech-stack changes across your market, then scores every contact 0–100 with a cited reason they're in-market now.

  • Up to 80 qualified leads per report
  • Per-contact fit score (0–100)
  • Buying trigger, cited and sourced
  • One-line outreach hook per contact
  • Delivered inside ARGUS in a matter of days
  • Download as PDF or DOCX

Company profiles & Moirai

Lynx

Lynx takes a list, yours or one from Argus, and profiles every company on it: who decides, what happened in the last 90 days, and the easiest way in. It keeps a people list that stays alive, and Moirai lets you ask the profiles anything. When one deal is worth more, it expands into a full strategic brief.

  • A full profile for every company on your list
  • The decision makers, by name and role
  • Stakeholder roles, LinkedIn, entry points
  • News from the last 90 days, each with its source and date
  • Moirai, a chat to ask your profiles anything
  • A people list that updates over time, exportable
  • For 30 days after delivery, ask the person who wrote your profiles anything about them

Team Workspace

Collab

Collab is where your team works: notes and pages, folders and files, spreadsheets with their full history, tasks, a shared calendar, a team chat. We set up the first version with you; after that it's yours to shape.

A separate product: use it on its own, or alongside our research. Your reports are delivered in ARGUS and LYNX, not here.

  • Shared notes, files and tasks in one hub
  • Shared calendar with month, week and agenda views
  • Tasks assigned to people, with due dates
  • A team chat
  • For every note and file, you choose who sees it
  • Organised the way your team already works
