01. Lead Lists

Argus

Argus builds precision lead lists from scratch, using your ICP as the only filter. Every contact arrives with a fit score from 0 to 100, a cited reason they're in-market, and a one-line way in, delivered as PDF and DOCX within 72 hours.

  • Up to 20 qualified leads per run
  • Per-contact fit score (0–100)
  • Buying trigger, cited and sourced
  • One-line outreach hook per contact
  • PDF + DOCX delivery within 72 hours
  • Exportable to any CRM or sequencer

Lynx

Lynx goes deeper on your five highest-priority accounts. Each gets a dedicated workspace: a complete decision-maker map, a curated 90-day signal feed, and a chat trained on everything we've gathered about that account.

  • Dedicated workspace per account (×5)
  • Full decision-maker org chart
  • Stakeholder roles, LinkedIn, entry points
  • 90-day news and intent signal feed
  • Contextual chat per account
