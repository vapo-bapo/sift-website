Lead Lists

Argus

Argus builds precision lead lists from your ICP alone, no generic scraping. It watches hiring, funding, leadership, and tech-stack changes across your market, then scores every contact 0–100 with a cited reason they're in-market now.

  • Up to 20 qualified leads per run
  • Per-contact fit score (0–100)
  • Buying trigger, cited and sourced
  • One-line outreach hook per contact
  • PDF + DOCX delivery within 72 hours
  • Exportable to any CRM or sequencer

Account Intelligence & Strategic Briefs

Lynx

Lynx opens a dedicated workspace on your five priority accounts: decision-maker map, 90-day signal feed, contextual chat. When one deal is worth more, it expands into a full strategic brief with a live analyst walkthrough.

  • Dedicated workspace per account (×5)
  • Full decision-maker org chart
  • Stakeholder roles, LinkedIn, entry points
  • 90-day news and intent signal feed
  • Contextual chat per account
  • Deep-dive strategic brief on any one target
  • Competitive analysis + direct analyst line for 30 days

Team Workspace

Collab

Collab is the shared workspace where every Argus list and Lynx brief lands, alongside your team's own notes, files, tasks, and calendar. Nothing gets buried in an inbox.

Included with every SIFT service, and also available on its own.

  • Shared notes, files and tasks in one hub
  • Calendar view synced to your outreach calendar
  • Task assignment per lead or account
  • Team chat scoped to each account
  • Every Argus and Lynx deliverable auto-filed
  • Fully configurable around how your team already works
